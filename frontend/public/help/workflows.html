End-to-end view

The typical brokered-funding case

  1. Receive and protectUpload the private ZIP to quarantine. The package starts a case record; it is not proof of funding.
  2. Review the caseCheck package health, compare claims and findings, and add cited amendments where needed.
  3. Confirm the receiptMatch exactly one independent provider credit by account, currency, amount, and reference.
  4. Record the broker decisionApprove, request information, or reject based on the evidence and required risk areas.
  5. Confirm and executeBalance payouts and fees to the receipt, authorize the exact plan, and execute once.
  6. Reconcile and closeConfirm final provider results, close the case, and retain the signed evidence bundle.
Each control has a different purpose. Package intake, funding match, broker approval, plan authorization, and execution are separate checkpoints. Completing one does not complete the next.

Roles at a glance

The application assists; the broker decides

The application helps you

  • Hold packages in quarantine while safety checks run.
  • Organize submitted claims, machine findings, and evidence.
  • Read provider observations and calculate the funding plan.
  • Preserve plan versions, approvals, and the audit history.

The broker is responsible for

  • Assessing the submitted claims and supporting evidence.
  • Clearing hard blocks with evidence or rejecting the case.
  • Recording the appropriate case outcome and factual reason.
  • Confirming the exact funding plan before authorization.
