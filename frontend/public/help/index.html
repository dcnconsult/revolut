Welcome

Get ready to begin

This guide is for the broker who reviews a client case and records the final human decision. Your access level may appear as Administrator in the application.

First, confirm the yellow Sandbox banner. You are in the right place when it says “REVOLUT SANDBOX · NO LIVE DATA.” If the banner is missing or different, pause and contact your administrator.

These opening checks take less than a minute:

  1. 1
    Open the private application address supplied by your administrator.
  2. 2
    Sign in with your own account so the case record correctly shows who completed the review.
  3. 3
    Enter the current six-digit authenticator code when requested. Save a recovery code for emergency one-time use only.
  4. 4
    Check the Operations card. Clear means the review can continue.
  5. 5
    Check the Backup card. Fresh is required before you authorize a funding plan.

You are ready when: you are signed in, the environment is Sandbox, Operations is Clear, and Backup is Fresh.

Sandbox means test-only. Do not use live money. Client case information may be used only when the named pilot data-handling approval allows it.

A quick daily routine

Complete the daily check

Use the status cards to make sure the broker desk is ready before you open or continue a case.

  1. Read the yellow environment banner.
  2. Confirm Operations: Clear.
  3. Confirm Backup: Fresh.
  4. Look for cases marked Quarantined, Intake Hold, Information Required, Partial, or Blocked.
  5. Record the date, time, your name, and whether the daily check passed.
Pause and ask for help if: Operations is Blocked, Backup is not Fresh, the Sandbox banner is missing, or a case has an unexplained Partial or Blocked execution.

The broker workflow

Move through the case in order

The case moves from receiving a file to broker review, decision, Sandbox testing, and closeout. The application keeps the record organised; you make the broker decision.

Important distinction: an uploaded ZIP creates a case record. It is not proof of incoming funds and it does not authorize a payout.

Open the high-level workflow map for a one-page visual view.

  1. 1
    Receive the case. Choose the private ZIP and select Upload to quarantine once.
  2. 2
    Check the file. Wait for the safety check, then read the safe file list and any questions.
  3. 3
    Complete the broker review. Compare submitted case details, workspace findings, and your professional review.
  4. 4
    Record checked details. If something needs correction, record what changed, where you checked it, and why.
  5. 5
    Confirm the Sandbox test receipt. Match the expected receipt to a result read from Revolut Sandbox.
  6. 6
    Record the broker decision. Approve, request information, or reject based on the evidence and required risk areas.
  7. 7
    Confirm payouts and fees. Customer payouts, broker fee, provider fees, reserve, and refund must equal the matched receipt.
  8. 8
    Authorize and execute. Confirm the exact funding plan, then complete the separate authorization and execution checks.
  9. 9
    Reconcile and close. Check the final result reported by Revolut Sandbox and retain the signed case record.
A clean ZIP can still be new to the workspace. If its layout is not recognised, read the safe file list and use Confirm real-case inputs for facts you checked independently. Do not guess from the file.

Evidence review

Respond to findings with checked information

A finding tells you what passed, what needs attention, or what prevents the case from moving forward.

  1. Read each finding and the related “what is needed next” instruction.
  2. Obtain a supporting record from an approved source.
  3. Add a recorded correction with the changed detail, reason, source, and where you checked it.
  4. Read the updated result.
  5. If a hard block remains, request the needed information or reject the case. A hard block cannot be accepted as risk.
The history stays clear: the original submitted detail remains in the record, and your recorded correction shows what changed and why.

Independent funding evidence

Confirm the matching Sandbox test receipt

The matching test receipt must be read from Revolut Sandbox, separately from the submitted ZIP.

  1. Confirm all other stop reasons are resolved.
  2. Select Create and match test credit only when the case is ready for that Sandbox step.
  3. Record the clearly labelled Sandbox test credit as a simulation, not real settlement.
  4. Confirm exactly one credit matches the Sandbox account, currency, full amount, and payment reference.
  5. Move forward when Funding says Matched.
Full amount, test-only: a broker-confirmed case uses its full confirmed amount within the approved Sandbox case limit. This is still a Sandbox test, never a live payment. If Sandbox limits or rejects it, keep that result in the case. Do not lower the amount yourself.
Pause the case when Funding says Possible Match, Unmatched, Awaiting Funds, Reversed, or Unclear. The receipt is not yet confirmed.

Broker decision

Record the appropriate case outcome

Choose the outcome that best reflects the evidence available now:

  • Approve when every required risk area passes, funding is Matched, and the purpose is understood.
  • Request information when specific evidence is missing or unclear and can be supplied safely.
  • Reject when the request cannot proceed or the evidence cannot safely resolve the concern.

Add a short, factual reason that another reviewer can follow. For example, name the evidence that supports the outcome instead of writing “looks fine” or “approved by system.” The broker owns the human decision.

Funding plan review

Confirm payouts, fees, reserve, and refund

The payment plan is the exact use of the matching Sandbox test receipt. Review every line, including small fees.

  1. Confirm the receipt amount and currency match the observed credit.
  2. Confirm each beneficiary name and payment destination.
  3. Confirm each customer payout.
  4. Confirm the broker fee and provider fees.
  5. Confirm any reserve or refund is explicit.
  6. Add every line. The plan total must equal the matched receipt exactly.
Matched receipt = customer payouts + broker fee + provider fees + reserve/refund

Final confirmation

Authorize the approved plan, then execute

Authorization applies to one exact version of the payment plan. Take your time and complete each confirmation once.

  1. Read the plan version and short plan reference shown by the application.
  2. Confirm no evidence, beneficiary, allocation, funding, or provider detail changed.
  3. Enter your password and a fresh authenticator code.
  4. Select Authorize once.
  5. Review the plan again before execution.
  6. Enter your password and a fresh code for the separate execution check.
  7. Select Execute once, then allow the provider result to return.
If the result is slow, pending, or unclear, pause here. Refresh or reconcile the existing request before taking any further action; do not select Execute again.

Case closeout

Confirm the Sandbox result and close the case

Reconcile checks what Revolut Sandbox actually completed before the case is closed. It checks the existing test; it does not send another payment.

  1. Select Reconcile only after a plan was executed and the result is not final.
  2. Confirm every submitted payout is Completed.
  3. Confirm no later payout was submitted after a failed, pending, reverted, declined, or unclear result.
  4. Continue reconciliation until the case says Reconciled. If it cannot reach a final state, pause and ask for support.
  5. Download the signed evidence bundle.
  6. Record the case reference, date, final state, and evidence filename.

The case is complete when: Case is Closed, Execution is Reconciled, and the signed evidence bundle is retained in the approved location.

Support

Pause safely and share the useful details

An error or unclear result does not need to be solved at the broker desk. Preserve the case as it is and send a short support note.

  1. Pause without retrying an authorization, execution, or submitted transfer.
  2. Write down the time, case reference, screen title, status, and the plain-language error.
  3. Do not include passwords, MFA codes, recovery codes, cookies, tokens, full account identifiers, balances, or package contents.
  4. Leave the case unchanged so the administrator can review the same record.
  5. Contact the named administrator through the approved support channel.

Good case handling

Protect the client, your account, and the case record

These boundaries keep the broker review reliable and preserve a clear audit history.

  • Use Revolut Sandbox only. The small Direct Transfer Test is a connection check; a broker-confirmed case may use its full confirmed test amount within the approved Sandbox case limit. It never moves live money.
  • Use client case information only after the named pilot data-handling and retention approval is confirmed.
  • Use only your own Sandbox credentials; do not use investor banking credentials.
  • Confirm an incoming test receipt through a result read from Revolut Sandbox, not through an uploaded ZIP.
  • Use only an existing, verified Sandbox counterparty or owned account.
  • Keep passwords, MFA and recovery codes, tokens, cookies, and secret files private.
  • Resolve a warning with a supporting record or the appropriate case decision; do not change a case simply to remove the warning.
  • Refresh or reconcile a test with an unclear outcome instead of retrying it.
  • Use this application only for Sandbox cases and test money.

Broker terminology in plain language

Broker glossary

Broker review
Your documented professional review of submitted case details, findings, and supporting records.
Case
The complete review record for one funding request.
Submitted case detail
Something the sender says is true. It is not proof.
Safe file list
The protected list of clean files in the ZIP. It helps you review a new or unfamiliar layout without treating it as payment instructions.
Supporting record
A record you checked independently to support or correct a case detail.
Finding
A problem, warning, or passed check produced during review.
Hard block
A concern that must be cleared with a supporting record or lead to rejection. It cannot be accepted as risk.
Recorded correction
A dated correction that leaves the original submitted detail visible.
Beneficiary
The person or organization intended to receive a payout.
Counterparty
A payment recipient already verified in Revolut Sandbox.
Sandbox result
Transaction information independently read from Revolut Sandbox.
Matching Sandbox test receipt
Exactly one Sandbox credit that matches the expected account, currency, full amount, and reference.
Payment plan
The exact list of customer payouts, broker fee, provider fees, reserve, and refund tied to one matching Sandbox test receipt.
Plan reference
A short identifier that shows the plan did not change since you reviewed it.
Reconcile
Refresh the existing case from Revolut Sandbox and confirm its final result. It does not send another payment.
Signed case record
The downloadable case history retained after reconciliation and closeout. The workspace may call this a “signed evidence bundle.”
Sandbox
A test environment. Its funds and identities are not real.

No matching guide section

Try a shorter word such as “funds,” “pending,” “upload,” or “backup.”
