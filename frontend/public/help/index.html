Welcome

Get ready to begin

This guide is for the broker who reviews the funding request and records the final human decision. Your access level may appear as Administrator in the application.

First, confirm the yellow Sandbox banner. You are in the right place when it says “REVOLUT SANDBOX · NO LIVE DATA.” If the banner is missing or different, pause and contact your administrator.

These opening checks take less than a minute:

  1. 1
    Open the private application address supplied by your administrator.
  2. 2
    Sign in with your own account so the case record correctly shows who completed the review.
  3. 3
    Enter the current six-digit authenticator code when requested. Save a recovery code for emergency one-time use only.
  4. 4
    Check the Operations card. Clear means the review can continue.
  5. 5
    Check the Backup card. Fresh is required before you authorize a funding plan.

You are ready when: you are signed in, the environment is Sandbox, Operations is Clear, and Backup is Fresh.

A quick daily routine

Complete the daily check

Use the status cards to make sure the broker desk is ready before you open or continue a case.

  1. Read the yellow environment banner.
  2. Confirm Operations: Clear.
  3. Confirm Backup: Fresh.
  4. Look for cases marked Quarantined, Intake Hold, Information Required, Partial, or Blocked.
  5. Record the date, time, your name, and whether the daily check passed.
Pause and ask for help if: Operations is Blocked, Backup is not Fresh, the Sandbox banner is missing, or a case has an unexplained Partial or Blocked execution.

The broker workflow

Move through the case in order

The case moves from intake to broker review, decision, execution, and closeout. The application helps organize the evidence and checks; you make the broker decision.

Important distinction: an uploaded package creates a case record. It is not proof of incoming funds and it does not authorize a payout.

Open the high-level workflow map for a one-page visual view.

  1. 1
    Receive the case. Choose the private ZIP and select Upload to quarantine once.
  2. 2
    Review package health. Wait for scanning and validation to finish.
  3. 3
    Complete the broker review. Compare submitted claims, machine findings, and your professional review.
  4. 4
    Add cited evidence. If a claim needs correction, record the reason, source, and evidence reference.
  5. 5
    Confirm the funding receipt. Match the expected receipt to an independent provider observation.
  6. 6
    Record the broker decision. Approve, request information, or reject based on the evidence and required risk areas.
  7. 7
    Confirm payouts and fees. Customer payouts, broker fee, provider fees, reserve, and refund must equal the matched receipt.
  8. 8
    Authorize and execute. Confirm the exact funding plan, then complete the separate authorization and execution checks.
  9. 9
    Reconcile and close. Confirm final provider results and retain the signed evidence bundle.

Evidence review

Clear findings with cited evidence

A finding tells you what passed, what needs attention, or what prevents the case from moving forward.

  1. Read each finding and the related “what is needed next” instruction.
  2. Obtain new evidence from an approved source.
  3. Add an amendment with the corrected value, reason, source, and evidence reference.
  4. Confirm the machine review runs again and read the updated result.
  5. If a hard block remains, request the needed information or reject the case. A hard block cannot be accepted as risk.
The history stays clear: the original claim remains in the record, and your amendment shows what changed and why.

Independent funding evidence

Confirm the matched funding receipt

The funding receipt must come from a provider observation, separate from the submitted package.

  1. Confirm all non-funding hard blocks are resolved.
  2. Select the funding refresh action to read the latest provider observations.
  3. For training, the clearly labeled Sandbox simulation option may be used. Record it as a simulation, not as real settlement.
  4. Confirm exactly one credit matches the provider account, currency, amount, and payment reference.
  5. Move forward when Funding says Matched.
Pause the case when Funding says Possible Match, Unmatched, Awaiting Funds, or Reversed. The receipt is not yet confirmed.

Broker decision

Record the appropriate case outcome

Choose the outcome that best reflects the evidence available now:

  • Approve when every required risk area passes, funding is Matched, and the purpose is understood.
  • Request information when specific evidence is missing or unclear and can be supplied safely.
  • Reject when the request cannot proceed or the evidence cannot safely resolve the concern.

Add a short, factual reason that another reviewer can follow. For example, name the evidence that supports the outcome instead of writing “looks fine” or “approved by system.” The broker owns the human decision.

Funding plan review

Confirm payouts, fees, reserve, and refund

The funding plan is the exact use of the matched receipt. Review every line, including small fees.

  1. Confirm the receipt amount and currency match the observed credit.
  2. Confirm each beneficiary name and payment destination.
  3. Confirm each customer payout.
  4. Confirm the broker fee and provider fees.
  5. Confirm any reserve or refund is explicit.
  6. Add every line. The plan total must equal the matched receipt exactly.
Matched receipt = customer payouts + broker fee + provider fees + reserve/refund

Final confirmation

Authorize the approved plan, then execute

Authorization applies to one exact version of the funding plan. Take your time and complete each confirmation once.

  1. Read the plan version and digest shown by the application.
  2. Confirm no evidence, beneficiary, allocation, funding, or provider detail changed.
  3. Type the exact authorization phrase shown.
  4. Enter your password and a fresh authenticator code.
  5. Select Authorize once.
  6. Review the plan again before execution.
  7. Enter the separate execution phrase, password, and fresh code.
  8. Select Execute once, then allow the provider result to return.
If the result is slow, pending, or unclear, pause here. Reconcile the existing request before taking any further action; do not select Execute again.

Case closeout

Confirm provider results and close the case

Reconciliation confirms what the provider actually completed before the case is closed.

  1. Select Reconcile for non-final provider results.
  2. Confirm every submitted payout is Completed.
  3. Confirm no later payout was submitted after a failed, pending, reverted, declined, or unclear result.
  4. Continue reconciliation until the case says Reconciled. If it cannot reach a final state, pause and ask for support.
  5. Download the signed evidence bundle.
  6. Record the case reference, date, final state, and evidence filename.

The case is complete when: Case is Closed, Execution is Reconciled, and the signed evidence bundle is retained in the approved location.

Support

Pause safely and share the useful details

An error or unclear result does not need to be solved at the broker desk. Preserve the case as it is and send a short support note.

  1. Pause without retrying an authorization, execution, or submitted transfer.
  2. Write down the time, case reference, screen title, status, and the plain-language error.
  3. Do not include passwords, MFA codes, recovery codes, cookies, tokens, full account identifiers, balances, or package contents.
  4. Leave the case unchanged so the administrator can review the same record.
  5. Contact the named administrator through the approved support channel.

Good case handling

Protect the client, your account, and the case record

These boundaries keep the broker review reliable and preserve a clear audit history.

  • Use Sandbox identities and low-value test amounts only.
  • Use only your own Sandbox credentials; do not use investor banking credentials.
  • Confirm incoming funds through provider observations, not through an uploaded package.
  • Use only an existing, verified Sandbox counterparty or owned account.
  • Keep passwords, MFA and recovery codes, tokens, cookies, and secret files private.
  • Resolve a warning with cited evidence or the appropriate case decision; do not change a case simply to remove the warning.
  • Reconcile a payment with an unclear outcome instead of retrying it.
  • Use this application only for Sandbox cases and test money.

Broker terminology in plain language

Broker glossary

Broker review
Your documented professional review of the submitted claims, findings, and supporting evidence.
Case
The complete review record for one funding request.
Claim
Something the sender says is true. A claim is not proof.
Evidence
A cited record used to support or correct a claim.
Finding
A problem, warning, or passed check produced during review.
Hard block
A concern that must be cleared with evidence or lead to rejection. It cannot be accepted as risk.
Amendment
A dated correction that leaves the original claim visible.
Beneficiary
The person or organization intended to receive a payout.
Counterparty
A payment recipient already verified in Revolut Sandbox.
Provider observation
Transaction information independently read from Revolut Sandbox.
Matched receipt
Exactly one provider credit that matches the expected account, currency, amount, and reference.
Funding plan
The exact list of customer payouts, broker fee, provider fees, reserve, and refund tied to one matched receipt.
Digest
A short fingerprint used to prove the plan did not change.
Reconcile
Ask the provider for the latest result and compare it with the case record.
Signed evidence bundle
The downloadable case history retained after reconciliation and closeout.
Sandbox
A test environment. Its funds and identities are not real.
